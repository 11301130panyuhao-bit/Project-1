🎾Court Compass: Plan Your Trip Around Tennis🎾

A concept website that helps travelling tennis players find public courts, plan their trip, and discover good food nearby. I designed it because I love tennis, I love travelling, and I could never find both in one place.

🔱The challenge🔱

I started playing tennis two years ago, and I have travelled to the USA, Japan and Australia. Every time I arrive in a new city, I want to play, but finding a court is hard.

The information is spread across map apps, local websites and social media. I often do not know if a court is public, if I need to book, or what the surface is. After the game, I also want to find great food nearby.

✳️The solution✳️

I designed a simple concept called Court Compass. It keeps everything a travelling player needs in one place:

  • Search by city to find nearby courts.
  • Court cards show the surface, public or private, lights and booking info.
  • A "Play + Eat" list shows good food spots close to each court.
  • A trip planner puts courts and meals into one easy timeline.

I started with quick sketches, then made wireframes in Figma for desktop and mobile. I used a clean layout, one bright accent colour and big buttons, so it is easy to use on a phone at the court.

💡Results and key outcomes💡

  • Defined one clear idea: a single place to find courts, plan trips and discover food.
  • Designed the main features: city search, court cards, a "Play + Eat" list and a trip planner.
  • Created a simple brand: a compass logo that shows the idea of finding your way to a court.
  • Planned a layout that works on desktop and mobile, so players can use it at the court.
  • Next step: test the idea with other tennis players and improve it.
